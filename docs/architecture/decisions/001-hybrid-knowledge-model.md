# ADR-001: Hybrid personal knowledge model

**Status:** Approved  
**Date:** 24 July 2026  
**Supersedes:** Treating all personal knowledge as generic profile facts

## Decision

Keep the existing profile-fact lifecycle for simple, stable statements. Use
small typed models for career modes, preferences and constraints, decision
policies, reusable evidence, skills, capability assessments, temporary state,
historical observations, coaching policies, decision priorities, and limited
working-style context.

Typed records share consistent lifecycle, provenance, confidence, temporal,
mode-scope, and tag metadata where applicable, but materially different records
are not forced into one universal table.

## Consequences

- Existing Phase 1 and Phase 2 work remains valid and is extended in place.
- Evidence is reusable and citable across analysis, CVs, writing, and
  interviews.
- Mutable capability and temporary state do not become permanent identity
  claims.
- A knowledge graph, arbitrary record schemas, and a user rules language are
  outside the approved architecture.
