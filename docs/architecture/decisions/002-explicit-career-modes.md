# ADR-002: Explicit career modes

**Status:** Approved  
**Date:** 24 July 2026  
**Supersedes:** One global policy and scoring context for every job

## Decision

Every job analysis must reference one explicitly selected career mode. The
initial modes are:

- **Primary career:** Frontend Engineer, Product Engineer, UX Engineer, Product
  Designer, UX Designer, then UI Designer, in that priority order.
- **Temporary income:** professional office or technology-adjacent income while
  the primary search continues, excluding the approved prohibited manual,
  retail, hospitality, delivery, warehouse, shop, supermarket, and caretaking
  categories.

The application must not infer or automatically switch mode from a job
description. Mode changes the policies and priorities selected for an analysis;
it does not rewrite the underlying profile.

## Consequences

- Primary and temporary objectives are never blended into one score.
- Temporary recommendations explain their limited purpose and opportunity
  cost.
- The initial UI exposes only these two modes; additional modes are deferred.
